### ■ 🚀 The AI/OCR Engine that "Reads" instead of just looking at images
The greatest strength of Aimeck15 is its high-performance OCR (Optical Character Recognition) engine. Unlike traditional macros that hunt for "image shapes," Aimeck15 literally "reads" the text on your screen. Font size changes, font updates, and background shifts won't stop you. It accurately pinpoints specified text strings like "OK" or "Purchase" and executes reliable, glitch-free clicks.

### ■ 📐 Environment-Agnostic "Smart Window Scaling"
No need to recreate your image templates every time you upgrade your PC or change monitor settings. Aimeck15 treats the target window as the recognition area and automatically registers the "Base Scale" internally. It handles resolution differences across environments from Full HD to 4K and various DPI scaling settings. Once created, your templates are reusable across any environment.

### ■ 🤖 "Natural Mouse" function that emulates human movement
Eliminate robotic straight-line movements and single-millisecond precision clicks. Aimeck15 highly simulates "natural trajectory" (curves) and random delays born from human hand movement. It reproduces mouse behavior that feels organic, with the fluidity and unpredictability that keep you safe from detection.

### ■ 🔥 Unleash Potential: Full Multi-threading & Expansion Optimization
Aimeck15 is designed to satisfy users who value PC specs. Upon startup, it automatically detects your CPU's expansion features (SIMD/AVX, etc.) to optimize and parallelize heavy image recognition tasks with fully multi-threaded execution. This allows you to achieve staggering performance exceeding "100 FPS," even in small-to-medium resolution environments. Experience a feather-light performance that unleashes the machine's true potential.

### ■ 👁️ 24/7 Monitoring: The reliability of "Rust & Slint UI"
